📷 OCR Scanner Lite+

OCR Receipt Scanner — Scan to Auto-Fill

Photograph any bill, receipt, or vendor invoice — BillBook's on-device OCR reads the text and offers to automatically fill in Amount, Date, Vendor Name, Bill Number, and GSTIN. Works completely offline. No API key or cloud service required.

ℹ️

When to use: Every time you receive a paper receipt or vendor bill. Instead of typing amounts and dates manually, just take a photo — OCR does the data entry for you. Works for expense receipts (petrol, utilities, supplies) and purchase bills from vendors. A clear, well-lit photo gives the best results.

1
Attach a Receipt Photo
On the Add Expense or New Purchase Bill screen, tap the Receipt card at the top. Choose Camera (take a new photo) or Gallery (pick an existing one). The image is attached and stored in your Google Drive backup.
2
Tap "Scan & Fill"
With the image attached, tap the Scan & Fill chip. The on-device OCR engine (Google ML Kit) reads the text — no internet needed. A loading indicator shows while processing (usually 1–3 seconds).
3
Review Extracted Fields
A Receipt Scan Results sheet appears showing every detected field with a checkbox beside it. Fields detected: Amount, Date, Vendor Name (expenses) plus Bill Number and GSTIN (purchase bills). Uncheck any field you don't want applied.
4
Apply & Verify
Tap Apply Selected Fields. The form fields fill instantly. Always glance at the filled values — OCR is very accurate but may occasionally misread a digit on blurry images. Edit any field as needed before saving.
  • Expenses: detects Amount, Date, Vendor Name — fills form with one tap
  • Purchase Bills: detects Vendor Name, Bill Number, Date, Total Amount, and GSTIN
  • On-device processing — fully offline, no data sent to any server
  • Per-field checkboxes — apply only the fields you want; existing form data is never overwritten
  • Supports common Indian receipt formats: DD/MM/YYYY dates, ₹ / Rs. amounts, 15-char GSTIN
  • Receipt photo stored in Google Drive backup alongside your expense/bill records
  • Benefits: 5× faster data entry; eliminate typos; build a complete digital receipt archive; CA-ready documentation with zero extra effort
💡

For best OCR results: photograph the receipt flat on a contrasting surface with good lighting. Hold the phone steady and make sure the entire receipt fits in frame. The "Total" or "Grand Total" line at the bottom gives the most reliable amount extraction.

⚠️

Always verify OCR-filled values before saving, especially amounts. Blurry images or stylised fonts on some receipts may result in imprecise text extraction. The original receipt photo is your ground truth.

Track every bill you receive from suppliers. Attach the bill photo, OCR-scan to auto-fill vendor details, record partial or full payments, and monitor everything you owe — payables in one place.

ℹ️

When to use: Any time you receive a bill from a supplier, contractor, or vendor. Keeping purchase bills in BillBook gives you a complete picture of payables alongside receivables — essential for accurate P&L, Cash Flow, and Input Tax Credit (ITC) reconciliation.

1
Scan or Enter the Bill
Tap Purchase Bills → +. Attach the bill photo with the Receipt card at the top, tap Scan & Fill to auto-fill Vendor, Bill No., Date, Amount, and GSTIN — or enter the details manually.
2
Add Line Items
Enter individual items with quantity, rate, and GST % for accurate ITC tracking. Or enter a single total-line if you don't need item-level detail.

Set Due Date & Reminder
Enter the payment due date and toggle Payment Reminder to get a push notification before it's due — never miss a supplier payment deadline.
4
Record Payments

  • Filter by status: All / Unpaid / Partial / Paid
  • Search by vendor name, bill number, or GSTIN
  • OCR auto-fills: Vendor Name, Bill Number, Date, Total, and GSTIN from the bill photo

  • Only GST-registered businesses with valid GSTIN are eligible

  • Generated tab shows all invoices with IRNs for easy reference
  • Benefits: stay GST compliant; IRN + QR auto-embedded, no manual copy-paste

❓ FAQ
Can I use BillBook without internet?
Yes. BillBook is offline-first — all data is stored locally. Create invoices, record payments and view reports with no internet. Changes sync to Google Drive when you reconnect.
What happens to my data if I lose my phone?
On Lite, Pro, Premium and Enterprise, all data is backed up continuously to your Google Drive (AES-256 encrypted). Sign in with the same Google account on a new device — everything restores automatically. Free plan is local only.
What happens when I hit the Free plan limit of 5 invoices?
On the 6th invoice attempt a paywall sheet appears showing the Lite plan details. Tap "Upgrade to Lite" or "Maybe later" — your draft is never lost.
Can my CA / accountant access my data?
Yes — on Premium invite them as a Viewer. They can browse all invoices and reports without being able to edit anything. On Pro, export the GST Report or P&L as PDF and share it.
Is GST data submitted to the government automatically?
No. BillBook is a private invoicing app. Use the exported GSTR-1 style report to file manually on the GST portal or hand it to your CA.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes — subscriptions are managed through Google Play or App Store. Cancel anytime; your plan stays active until the end of the billing period. Data is never deleted.
How do I add my team members / employees?
Go to Settings → Manage Team → + Invite Employee. A QR code is generated — the employee scans it from their own device using BillBook's pairing screen. No password sharing needed. Team management requires the Premium plan (up to 5 members) or Enterprise (unlimited).
What is a Credit Note and when should I issue one?
A Credit Note is a formal document that reduces or reverses an existing invoice — used for returned goods, billing corrections, or cancelled services. Open the original invoice → tap ⋮ → Issue Credit Note. Never delete an invoice to fix a mistake; a credit note keeps your audit trail clean and your GST records accurate. Requires Pro+.
How do I invoice in USD or other foreign currencies?
On the invoice creation screen, tap the currency symbol (₹) next to the total and select any currency — USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, and 30+ more. BillBook shows the live indicative exchange rate and converts the amount to your base currency in P&L reports. Multi-currency invoicing requires Pro+.
How does inventory tracking work — will stock reduce automatically?
Yes. Enable inventory tracking per item under Settings → Items & Services → Edit Item → Track Inventory. Set the opening quantity and low-stock threshold. Every time you save a sent invoice containing that item, BillBook deducts the quantity automatically. The Inventory screen shows current stock, low-stock items (amber) and out-of-stock items (red) at a glance. Requires Pro+.
How do I switch the app language to Hindi or Gujarati?
Go to Settings → Language and select your preferred language. The entire app switches instantly — menus, labels, and invoice PDFs all render in the chosen language. BillBook supports English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, and Telugu. Language switching is available on all plans at no extra cost.
How does the OCR Receipt Scanner work and what does it detect?
The OCR scanner uses Google ML Kit, an on-device text recognition engine — no internet is needed and no data leaves your phone. Take a photo of any receipt or vendor bill using the Receipt card on the Add Expense or New Purchase Bill screen, then tap Scan & Fill. For expenses it detects: Amount, Date, and Vendor Name. For purchase bills it also detects: Bill Number and GSTIN. Each detected field appears in a confirmation sheet with a checkbox — you choose which fields to apply before anything is written to the form.
Why did OCR not detect my receipt correctly?
OCR accuracy depends on image quality. For best results: photograph in good lighting, hold the phone steady, and make sure the entire receipt fits in frame. Blurry, crumpled, or very dark receipts may give partial results. You can always fill in the remaining fields manually — the photo is still attached and stored in your Google Drive backup for your CA's reference.
Do my settings (POS, Purchase Bills, dark mode) reset after an update?
No. All preferences are saved permanently in your local storage and Google Drive backup. Enabling POS, enabling Purchase Bills, choosing dark mode, setting a custom thank-you message — all of these persist across app restarts, phone reboots, and app updates. On Lite+ with Google Drive sync enabled, preferences also restore automatically when you sign in on a new device.
Will the invoice automatically move to "Paid" after multiple partial payments?
Yes, automatically. Each time you record a payment (partial or full), BillBook recalculates the total paid amount. The moment the sum of all payments covers the invoice total, the invoice status changes to Paid without any manual action needed. This works across both the invoice detail screen and the invoice list screen status sheet.
