Series counters are atomic: Even if two employees create invoices at the same moment, numbers never duplicate. BillBook uses Firestore transactions to guarantee unique, sequential numbers across all devices.

The Invoice Lifecycle

Every invoice follows a clear, traceable path. You always know what stage it's in — and BillBook prompts the right action at each step.

Draft

Created but not sent. Edit freely, no number assigned yet.

Pending Approval Optional

Staff-submitted invoices wait here for manager review. You (or a Manager) approve or reject with notes.

Sent / Unpaid

Invoice shared with client. Series number locked. Reminder schedule activates automatically.

Overdue

Past due date, still unpaid. Shows in red on dashboard. Late fee can be added if configured.

Paid / Partially Paid

Record payment(s). Partial payments tracked individually. Fully paid leaves the outstanding list.

Employee Management

Add your team members securely via QR code pairing. Assign roles that precisely control what each person can see and do.

Secure QR Pairing

No shared passwords. You show a QR code from your phone — the employee scans it on theirs. The connection is instant, encrypted, and fully in your control.

1

Add Employee

Settings → Employees → Add. Enter their name and assign a role (Manager, Staff, or Viewer).

2

Show QR Code

BillBook generates a secure QR code. Display it on your phone screen.

3

Employee Scans

On their phone, employee opens BillBook → Pair with owner → scans your QR. Done!

4

Instant Access

Employee now works on your data with exactly the permissions you set. You can revoke at any time.

🔐 Revoke Instantly

Delete or deactivate an employee from Settings → Employees. Their access is cut within 60 seconds — even if they're mid-session. No waiting for them to disconnect.

What happens on revocation:
✓ Firestore record removed / deactivated
✓ Next sync attempt detects revocation
✓ Employee app shows "Access removed" dialog
✓ Employee data cleared from their device
✓ All their session data purged

Settings — Explained

Everything in Settings is yours to customise. Here's what each section does.

🏢

Business Profile

Logo, legal name, address, GSTIN, PAN, bank details, digital signature, terms & conditions, and footer text. Printed on every PDF.

🧾

Invoice Settings

Series prefix, starting number, PDF template, show/hide fields (PO number, due date, HSN codes), default notes, and approval workflow on/off.

🏛️

GST Setup

Registered / unregistered, state code, composition scheme, reverse charge, and e-invoicing toggle. Drives all GST calculations app-wide.

💳

Payment Methods

Add UPI (auto-generates QR), bank account, cash. Multiple methods supported. Each method has an optional "default" flag per client.

💬

Message Templates

Customise the WhatsApp message sent with each invoice, quotation, and reminder. Use {name}, {amount}, {due_date} placeholders.

⏰

Reminder Settings

Set how many days before/after due date each reminder fires. Enable/disable reminder schedule globally or per invoice.

📦

Services & Items

Your product/service catalogue. Set name, rate, tax %, HSN/SAC code, unit, category, and whether to track stock with a low-stock threshold.

⚠️

Late Fee Settings

Choose a fixed or % late fee. Set the grace period (days after due date). Fee is added to reminder messages and shown on overdue invoices.

👥

Employees

Add, edit, deactivate, or delete team members. Shows paired device status and role. Delete removes access within 60 seconds on the employee's device.

🌍

Language & Region

Switch the app language (English, Hindi, and more). Currency, date format, and number format follow the locale automatically.

🎨

Appearance

Light / Dark / System theme. BillBook respects your system-level dark mode setting automatically.

Plan & Billing

Pro Tips

Owner's Playbook

Hard-won lessons from power users — save yourself months of discovery.

🎯

Set up your item catalogue first

Before creating your first invoice, go to Settings → Services & Items and add all your products and services with their rates and HSN codes. Creating an invoice takes seconds when items are pre-loaded.

⚡

Use recurring for retainer clients

If you bill the same client the same amount monthly, set it as recurring. BillBook creates the invoice automatically — you just approve and share.

📊

Check age-wise report weekly

The age-wise report takes 30 seconds to read and tells you exactly who owes you money and for how long. Run it every Monday as a collections habit.

👥

Use roles, not shared logins

Never share your Google login with staff. QR-pair each employee with the right role. If someone leaves, you revoke in one tap — they lose access immediately.

🔁

Quotation → Invoice in one tap

Never re-enter a quote as an invoice. Create the quotation, share it, get client approval, then convert. Line items, amounts, and client details all carry over.

📱

Your data is always yours

BillBook stores everything encrypted in your own Google Drive. Even if the app is discontinued, your data is safe. You own it — we just organise it.
