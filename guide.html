Scan Any Receipt — Auto-Fill the Entire Form

Photograph a bill, receipt, or vendor invoice. On-device OCR (Google ML Kit) reads the text and offers to fill Amount, Date, Vendor, Bill Number, and GSTIN. No internet needed — runs entirely on your device.

  • Expenses: detects Amount, Date, and Vendor Name
  • Purchase Bills: also detects Bill Number and GSTIN
  • Per-field checkboxes — apply only what you want
  • Supports Indian receipt formats: DD/MM/YYYY, ₹/Rs., 15-char GSTIN
  • Receipt photo stored in Google Drive alongside the record
  • Offline — no data sent to any server
  • Works in under 3 seconds on any modern Android or iPhone
